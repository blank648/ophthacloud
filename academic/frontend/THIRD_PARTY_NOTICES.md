## Modifications in `ophthacloud-mascot.svg`

- The SVG's complete chart geometry was extracted from the downloaded source; all **82 original path data (`d`) values**, the white paper rectangle and source-space transforms are preserved. The original optotypes, acuity labels, numbers and two divider-bar shapes are included, not recreated using a font.
- The native page size is 1303.2626 × 1624.4054 SVG units. The chart is scaled uniformly to approximately 316.107 × 394 units in the mascot, preserving its original aspect ratio (no stretching or cropping of the source lettering).
- The page is centred beneath the existing beak grip. Its width is increased relative to the narrower placeholder on the approved mascot so the source aspect ratio and previous chart height can both be maintained.
- For the approved OphthaCloud palette, the original black fills and strokes are recolored to `#2E333C`; the original green and red divider bars are recolored to `#F48FB1` and `#F8BBD0` respectively. The paper remains `#FFFFFF`. The source geometry and stroke widths are unchanged.
- The chart is layered behind the beak and approved head and in front of the eagle's body. All source rendering elements use local vector geometry. Original editor-specific non-rendering metadata and unused definitions are removed, and IDs are prefixed `oc-mascot-chart-` to prevent collisions. Attribution and license information are included in SVG metadata.

The integrated chart is an illustrative mascot element, not a calibrated clinical visual-acuity test.
